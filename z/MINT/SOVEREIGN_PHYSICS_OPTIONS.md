## 🏹 2. Options Execution: The "Put Option" Guide

A **Put Option** is a "Bet" that the market will go down. It gives you the Right to Sell a stock at a fixed price (**The Strike Price**). As the market falls, the value of your Put Option **Increases Exponentially.**
