1.  **The Engine:** You have your AI generate 100,000 different Rust solutions to technical problems.

3.  **The Training:** You discard the failures. You only train the AI on the **perfectly compiling, clippy-clean solutions.** 
4.  **The Result:** Your engine becomes "Zero-Error" because it was only ever trained on success.
