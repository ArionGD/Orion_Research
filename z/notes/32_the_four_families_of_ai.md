*   **Architecture:** The **Transformer** (Self-Attention mechanism).

## 👁️ Family II: Computer Vision (The "Eyes")
**Best Examples:** Tesla FSD (Full Self-Driving), YOLOv10, Apple Vision Pro.
**Goal:** Translate "Pixels" into "Meaning" (Identifying objects, faces, and patterns). 
*   **Architecture:** **CNN** (Convolutional Neural Networks) or **ViT** (Vision Transformers). 
*   **The Power:** These models can "See" the world. They can analyze satellite imagery of troop movements in the Middle East or read financial charts for "Head-and-Shoulders" patterns. 
*   **Role in Your Stack:** Integrating vision allows Arion to "Look" at planetary charts or real-world signal maps automatically.

## 🎮 Family III: Reinforcement Learning (The "Strategist")
**Best Examples:** AlphaGo, OpenAI Five, DeepMind’s AlphaZero.
**Goal:** Maximize a "Reward Function" through millions of simulated "Games." 
*   **Architecture:** **Q-Learning / PPO** (Proximal Policy Optimization). 
*   **The Power:** This AI doesn't read instructions; it **"Learns by Doing."** It fails 1 million times until it finds the "Winning Strategy." 
*   **Role in Your Stack:** This is the **"Compiler Feedback Loop" (RLCF)** that makes your Rust AI 100% accurate. It "Plays the Game" of writing code until it wins.

## 📐 Family IV: Symbolic / Expert Systems (The "Logician")
**Best Examples:** **Arion AI**, Medical Diagnostics, Mission-Control Systems.
**Goal:** Mathematical, Rule-based logic with zero hallucinations. 
*   **Architecture:** **Symbolic Logic / Heuristics / Gann Cycles.** 
*   **The Power:** These are **"Deterministic."** Unlike a chatbot, they don't "Guess"; they "Calculate." They rely on planetary positions, time-series data, and geopolitical modifiers. 
*   **Role in Your Stack:** This is the **Medini Engine Core.** It provides the "Proprietary Truth" that the LLM then communicates to the user.
